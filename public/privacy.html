Privacy

Poopy Bird watches your face to fly the bird, and it does all of that on your device. Nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your camera

The video from your camera is analysed in your browser (or in the Android app, on your phone). No frame, picture or measurement of your face is uploaded.

Pictures of you

During a run the game takes a few pictures: your most strained face, and the photos the paparazzi and the wedding photographer take. They are kept in memory, shown on the game-over screen, and gone when you play again or close the page. They leave your device only if you download or share them yourself.

Recognising returning players

So that a returning player can skip calibration, the game remembers a few numbers describing each face that calibrated (face descriptors, which count as biometric data). They stay in this browser tab and are deleted when you close it. Settings → Faces → Forget all deletes them at once.

What is saved on your device

Your settings, your best score and your calibration (statistics of your expressions, not pictures) are saved in your browser's local storage, so they are still there next time. Clearing the site's data removes them.

The website

poopy-bird.com only sends the game's files to your browser. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no third-party services, and it keeps no record of your visits. Like any website, the server sees your IP address while it sends you the files. The server is rented from Hetzner Online GmbH.
